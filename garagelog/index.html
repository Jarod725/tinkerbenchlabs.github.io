Built for the real world

GarageLog is the no-fuss maintenance tracker built for every vehicle in your life. Log oil changes, tire rotations, brake jobs, and dozens more service types. Set smart reminders by mileage, time, or both. GarageLog watches the clock and the odometer so you don't have to.

What's inside

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

🔒

No account required

All your data lives on your device. Nothing goes to our servers — ever. Optional iCloud sync (Pro) keeps your devices in step via your own Apple account.

⏰

Smart reminders

Set reminders by mileage, elapsed time, or both. GarageLog estimates your current odometer between fill-ups and alerts you before the interval is up — not after.

🚗

Every vehicle type

Cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats, ATVs, tractors, generators, mowers, trailers, and more. GarageLog handles 15 vehicle categories with category-aware service types and specs.

📋

30+ built-in service types

Oil changes, tire rotations, brake service, fluid checks, and dozens more — each with the right fields pre-built. Add custom event types for anything else.

📷

Photo receipts

Attach a photo of your receipt to any service log entry. Keeps everything in one place for warranty claims, resale, or peace of mind.

✨

GarageLog Pro

One-time purchase. Unlocks iCloud sync across your devices, removes ads, and supports continued development. No subscription, no recurring charges.
